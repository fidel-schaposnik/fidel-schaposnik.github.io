    leek pie

    Another simple pie, perfect for a quick weekday lunch.

    • Prep: 30 min.
    • Cooking: 20 min.
    • Makes: 1 pie (6 to 8 portions)

    Ingredients

    • 1 basic savory pie crust dough
    • 800 g leeks, white and light green parts sliced to get approximately 500 g
    • 1 large onion, finely chopped (about 250 g)
    • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
    • 4 tbsp olive oil
    • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
    • 250 g soft cheese or cottage cheese, cubed
    • salt and pepper
    • butter, for greasing the pie tin

    Method

    1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.
       
    2. Heat the olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the crushed garlic and cook for a few seconds until fragrant. Avoid burning the garlic or it will become bitter.
       
    3. Add the chopped onion to the pan and mix well. Cook for about 10 minutes, stirring every once in a while, until the onion starts to turn transparent.
       
    4. Incorporate the sliced leeks, a pinch of salt and a good grind of pepper. Cook for about 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until the onion is fully caramelized and the leeks start to char a little. Remove from the heat.
       
    5. While the filling is cooling down, butter a 26 cm pie tin with high sides. Using a rolling pin, stretch your dough to the tin’s size. You may need to sprinkle a bit of flour on your work surface to avoid it from sticking, but don’t overdo it or it will become harder to stretch. Place the stretched dough over the pie tin, going slightly over the borders.
       
    6. Add the beaten eggs and cheese to the leek and onion mixture. Mix everything well, then transfer to the pie tin and distribute evenly over the dough.
       
    7. Cook for about 20 minutes, until the crust starts to brown and the cheese is melted.
